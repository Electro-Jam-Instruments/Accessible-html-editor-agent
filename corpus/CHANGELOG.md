# Corpus changelog

Every accepted row change is recorded here against a corpus version, whichever way a
contest lands — a citation to a `CAN-…` id at a given corpus version must keep meaning
what it meant. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) §3.

## corpus-v1 — 2026-08-31

Initial published state: 218 canonical rows ([`canonical.md`](canonical.md)) merged from
the source-read inventories of Open Notebook (83 rows), Lexical (201) and CKEditor 5
(302), plus the Tiptap inventory (123 rows, measured 2026-08-30, not yet merged into the
canonical set). Corrections made during development (C1–C13) are recorded inline in the
rows they amend and in [`docs/evidence.md`](../docs/evidence.md).
